Privacy in plain words

Last updated: November 2026

The short version: Mochi's House has no ads, no tracking and no analytics, and sets no cookies. Without an account, everything stays in your browser. With an account, your planner is kept in our database so it can sync, only you can see it, and you can delete it any time.

Who runs it

Mochi's House is a small independent project run by Alexandra. Questions about your data, or anything else: email help@mochishouse.app, or use Settings → Help in the app.

Without an account

Your planner, recipes, notes, moods and settings are saved in your browser's own storage on your device. They never leave it, unless you download a backup file yourself. Clearing your browser's site data deletes them.

With an account (sync)

  • Your email address and password. Used only to sign you in and to send sign-in or password emails. Passwords are stored scrambled (hashed), never readable.
  • Your planner. Everything you'd otherwise keep in the browser (assignments, grades, recipes, chores, books, notes, moods, sleep, settings) is copied to our database so your devices stay in sync.
  • Your files. Pattern PDFs and pictures you add to notes, in a private folder only your account can open.
  • Mochi's Land. If you add neighbours, they can see your house name, your pet, its outfit and your house colours, plus notes, treats and pets you send them. Nothing else from your planner.

Our database and sign-in run on Supabase. The rules in the database only let each account read and change its own data.

Feedback

When you send feedback we get your message, the app version and, only if you type one, an email address to reply to.

Outside services the app talks to

  • Open-Meteo (weather): the city you search for, and its rounded location to fetch the forecast.
  • Open Library (books): what you type when you search for a book, and the book covers it shows.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website, and like any web host it sees visitors' IP addresses in its server logs.
  • Spotify (desktop app only): the app asks the Spotify app on your own computer what's playing. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Fonts, the sign-in library and the PDF reader are served from this website itself, so loading the page doesn't tell any other company you visited.

Cookies

Mochi's House sets no cookies. It uses your browser's storage only for things it needs to work: your saved planner and keeping you signed in. That's why there's no cookie banner.

Your choices and rights

  • See or take your data: Settings → Account & sync → Download a backup gives you everything in one file.
  • Delete it: Settings → Account & sync → Delete my account deletes your account and everything synced to it straight away.
  • Correct it: change anything in the app, or email help@mochishouse.app.
  • If you live in the EU you can also complain to your data protection authority (in Austria, the Datenschutzbehörde).

We keep your synced data while you have an account and delete it when you delete the account.
