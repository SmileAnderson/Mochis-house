                          Classes from Apple Calendar

                          Your days & term

                          Sundays are always free. Days with 4+ hours of classes get half the usual study time.

                          Your term or year

                          The bar at the top of the planner runs from the start to the end. Leave the dates empty to use the whole calendar year.

                          Days off

                          Breaks like a reading week or a holiday. They show on the bar; tick "keep these days free" if you don't want any sessions planned on them.

                          Welcome home

                          A cosy planner where every part of life is a room: a study for uni, a kitchen for meals, a laundry room for chores, a bedroom for sleep and moods. Set up your house in a minute. You can change all of it later.
